Main value proposition

"Understand your business finances, predict what comes next, and make better decisions with an AI copilot."

Module C: Cash-Flow Management and Forecasting

Create a dedicated cash-flow workspace.

Features:

  • Historical cash inflows and outflows.

  • Daily, weekly, and monthly views.

  • Expected customer payments.

  • Upcoming supplier payments.

  • Recurring operating costs.

  • Projected closing cash balance.

  • Estimated cash-shortage dates.

  • Cash-flow forecasting for 30, 60, and 90 days.

  • Best-case, base-case, and stress-case scenarios.

  • Interactive forecast charts.

  • Explanation of the factors influencing each forecast.

Forecasting logic

Start with a transparent, reliable baseline using historical transactions, scheduled receivables, scheduled payables, and opening cash balance.

Use this fundamental relationship:

Closing cash = Opening cash + Cash inflows − Cash outflows.

Forecasts should account for timing, not just aggregate totals.

If historical data is insufficient, explicitly label the forecast as provisional and explain why.

Do not fabricate a sophisticated machine-learning model. A deterministic baseline is acceptable for the MVP; an optional forecasting model may be added if there is enough data to validate it.

Scenario simulator

Let users change assumptions such as:

  • Revenue growth or decline.

  • Expected customer-payment delays.

  • Expense increases.

  • Supplier-payment timing.

  • Hiring costs.

  • A proposed new business expense.

Immediately recalculate the scenario and visualize the effect on future cash balances.

Show which assumptions were changed and how they affected the outcome.

  • Organization-level authorization.

  • Server-side secret management.

  • Input validation.

  • Safe file handling.
