# Finora user guide

Use synthetic examples for demonstrations. Recorded cash is not a verified bank balance. The default reporting snapshot is 2026-10-13.

## One connected business story

1. Open **Home**, select the intended business and check the snapshot, upcoming supplier obligations and cash estimate. Open the safe-to-spend explanation: preserve the owner’s emergency buffer and inspect the forecast low point.
2. Open **Record**. Add money received/spent only for an actual recorded event. For a supported text PDF, choose the file, inspect source evidence, edit missing/wrong values, confirm the payment status and approve posting. Upload alone does not establish payment. Suspected duplicates must be reviewed.
3. Open **More → Invoices** for customer invoices and supplier bills. Link a payment using the settlement workflow; do not separately add the same payment again. **Collect payments** supports notes, promised dates and reminder drafts; it sends no message.
4. Open **Forecast** for 30/60/90-day assumptions. Test a customer delay and inspect low-point date, obligations, reserve gap and uncertainty. These are forecasts, not ledger edits.
5. Open **Cash Rescue Planner** to compare bounded scenarios. Supplier deferral, accelerated collections and financing remain assumptions until agreed/executed outside the app. Save a **Decision Receipt** to retain the reasoning, not to apply a payment or loan.
6. Open **Assistant**. Ask a question, inspect the supporting figures and distinguish calculated responses from live provider explanations. Check connection/privacy and consent before live AI. The Start conversation action only focuses the composer.
7. Open **Account** for login, permitted business access and synchronization state. Existing browser examples are not automatically replaced on login. Review explicit cloud initialization/import choices and export backups before migration.

## More: all twenty tools

| Group | Tool routes and purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Records & documents | `pdfreceipts`: supported PDF review; `imports`: statement import; `fapiao`: supporting invoice/document review, not official verification; `reconcile`: matching and exceptions |
| Payments & spending | `invoices`: customer invoices/supplier bills; `collections`: follow-up; `expenses`: recorded spending; `financing`: hypothetical funding costs |
| Planning & decisions | `rescue`: cash responses; `actionplan`: proposed actions; `weekly`: weekly priorities; `options`: scenario comparison |
| Insights & assurance | `ai`: provider/business AI workspace; `reports`: performance; `quality`: confidence and evidence; `receipts`: saved Decision Receipts; `risk`: warning indicators |
| Business & settings | `setup`: business profile; `account`: identity/roles/sync; `settings`: appearance, data and backups |

The actual visible labels follow the selected English/Simplified Chinese language. Search is restricted to the selected directory category. The guide supports chapters, manual progression and exit; navigating a tour must not post transactions or discard a draft. Viewers have read-only access; accountants and owners remain constrained by the account rules.

Light is the current default; explicit dark preference is remembered. Reduced motion uses still dragon artwork. The dragon replay is decorative and does not change financial records.
