Source, read 19 October 2026: https://ird.oou.cmu.ac.th/2026-shenzhen-international-fintech-competition/

## Why this project fits

The prototype helps SME owners record payments, review documents, forecast cash, examine evidence and compare responses. Optional AI explains validated read-only tool outputs while the owner retains financial control. Planning is distinct from actual payment, bank verification, official invoice verification and lending approval.
